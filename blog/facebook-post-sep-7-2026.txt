🎮 PnP projects from the Facebook group today!

🌾 Of Wheat & Wonders by Robin Metz (pagepunk) — Gamefound, launching Sep 8
A dynamic solo civilization game with an online configurator. Generate maps, gather resources, unlock advancements across a 15-year reign. Runs through Oct 7.
https://gamefound.com/en/projects/beaverlicious-games/of-wheat-wonders-the-little-solitaire-civilization-game-dynamic-pnp-of-the-month-gamebook

🗡️ Forgotten Paths by Spiros Kallos (Tabletop for World) — Gamefound, launching Sep 8
Solo PnP dungeon crawler as a Mistwalker. Complete quests, unlock talents, let the Storybook guide you. Express campaign — less than a day to go!
https://gamefound.com/en/projects/tabletop-for-world/forgotten-paths

⚔️ Steel Whisper by Radek Ignatów — Kickstarter, preview
Compact solo mech assault game. Draw the enemy in, inflict damage, survive for extraction. Two weeks to launch.
https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/105281170/steel-whisper

🎲 Roll Breakers by Alexz Martínez (RAWR! Games) — Kickstarter, preview
"If you liked Balatro, check out Roll Breakers." 6 classes, 216 skills, roguelike progression. Numbers-up fun.
https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/rawrgames/roll-breakers

🏰 Lords of the Dungeon by Keepers of Fun — Kickstarter, preview
Roll-and-write dungeon builder with a Random Production mechanism. Tutorial video is up, 3 weeks to launch.
