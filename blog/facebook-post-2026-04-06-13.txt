What's new on the Launchpad this week?

We added a strong batch of new entries: Forgetify, Dino Farmers, Bananarchy, Play With Fire, Sole Survivor, Galen's Games Mint Tin Series 2, Shinobi Spy & Supply, and What If...WARS. Nice mix too. Not all chasing the same lane, which makes the site a lot more interesting to browse.

On the live side, Cat Saga and Dino Farmers feel the most urgent right now. BYTE: SHIFT is now on a real campaign schedule instead of sitting in preview, Galen's Games Mint Tin Series 2 is a clean fit for fans of small fast 1-2 player games, and Hidden Realms is still the biggest adventure-style project on the board.

Looking ahead, the upcoming section is quietly getting stronger too. Sole Survivor, Play With Fire, Shinobi Spy & Supply, and Forgetify give the next stretch of Launchpad a lot more personality than the usual parade of samey projects.
