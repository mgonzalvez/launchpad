What's new on the Launchpad this week?

A few projects deserve immediate attention. Forage + Honeypot (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/flatoutgames/forage-honeypot/description) is basically at the wire, so if you were interested, now is the time. Fort Douaumont (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/dadu/fort-douaumont) is also getting close enough that it should be on your short list.

We also added a nice batch of new entries to the site: Dino Farmers (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/nukazombee/dino-farmers), Bananarchy (https://bananarchylaunch.pickupandplaygames.com/), Play With Fire (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/umbrapublishing/play-with-fire-the-card-game), Sole Survivor (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/papertigers/sole-survivor-0), Forgetify (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/nonymousgames/forgetify-recall-recite-repeat-0), Path of Bones (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/printandgame/path-of-bones-print-and-play-pnp-card-game-pdf?ref=discovery&term=print%20and%20play%20games&total_hits=491&category_id=34), and What If...WARS (https://site.wargamevault.com/product/560851/WHAT-IFWars-WWII-Series?affiliate_id=3623781). Good mix this week too, not just more of the same. There's some real variety there.

On the live side, Hidden Realms: The Mummy's Tomb (https://gamefound.com/en/projects/tabletop-for-world/hidden-realms-the-mummys-tomb) is now up and running, Hexoplanets (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/fullsauna/hexoplanets/) is still live, Cat Saga (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/rollingrhinogames/catsaga) still has runway, and What If...WARS (https://site.wargamevault.com/product/560851/WHAT-IFWars-WWII-Series?affiliate_id=3623781) is there for the wargame crowd as a limited-time promo.

On the upcoming side, Dino Farmers (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/nukazombee/dino-farmers) looks like the next one most worth watching, and Sole Survivor (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/papertigers/sole-survivor-0), Play With Fire (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/umbrapublishing/play-with-fire-the-card-game), and Forgetify (https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/nonymousgames/forgetify-recall-recite-repeat-0) all help make the next stretch of Launchpad feel pretty healthy.
