What's New on the Launchpad This Week of March 9-15, 2026

KING is the live campaign with the clearest urgency in this week's thread. Radek Ignatow says it is now halfway through its Kickstarter run, which means this is the stretch where a good campaign either sharpens its momentum or gets taken for granted. If you like strategic, puzzle-forward print-and-play designs, this is the one in this week's thread that most clearly deserves active backing attention right now.

KING
https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/radek-ignatow/king-0

Also live and newly added to Launchpad since the last roundup: HEX FACTOR, Stitchery Faire, Hexoplanets, and LUDOS: The ancient games collection - Europe. That is a strong spread: fast abstract competition, cozy solo storytelling, dice-and-pencil terraforming, and a pocketable historical games collection. If you have not checked Launchpad in a few days, those are the new live additions worth scanning first.

Coming soon projects with the strongest signals: Hidden Realms: The Mummy's Tomb is now nine days from launch, and the creator is already responding to community feedback with a new interacting competitive mode variant and a fourth map as stretch goals. That is the kind of pre-launch update I like to see because it suggests the creator is listening early rather than scrambling late. Fort Douaumont also continues to make a strong case for itself as a small-footprint tactical design with real tension, while Bravery remains one to watch for survival game fans.

Preview watch: Mïnomancer - Roll em Bones! is the standout preview project in this week's thread. A solo roll-and-write dungeon crawler built around raising a skeletal army, arranging polyomino formations, and pushing through multi-level dungeon runs toward one of more than fifteen bosses is a strong pitch, and it feels distinct enough to be worth following closely before dates lock in.

Mïnomancer - Roll em Bones!
https://gamefound.com/en/projects/beaverlicious-games/minomancer---the-roll--write-dungeon-crawler-dynamic-pnp-of-the-month--gamebook

Not crowdfunding, but still worth your attention: Grimm World - Isegrim, the Wolf is now available as a final free download with finished illustrations and proofreading complete. Perfect 10 Fruit Salad is a family-weight Forever Free print-and-play entry for the 2026 Children & Family Game Design Contest on BoardGameGeek. And Space Slug Games is offering a free PnP while building interest for the later Station Decimus Kickstarter. None of those are current crowdfunding campaigns, but they are exactly the kind of adjacent finds this community should know about.

Bottom line: KING is the live campaign in this week's thread with the strongest immediate call to action, Hidden Realms and Fort Douaumont are the coming-soon projects with the best early momentum, and Mïnomancer is the preview project most likely to earn a bigger audience if people keep following and sharing it. On top of that, the thread also delivered a few very solid non-crowdfunding finds, which is part of what makes these weekly promotion posts worth reading in the first place.
