Facebook roundup from this week's PnP Hideaway group posts! 🎲

Here are all the PnP crowdfunding projects that showed up in the group over the last few days — cross-checked against the Launchpad:

🦴 Mïnomancer: Roll em Bones! by Robin Metz (pagepunk) — Gamefound, launched May 5. Solo roll-and-write dungeon crawler. Polyomino formations, 15+ bosses, 8 skeleton classes. One of the most anticipated launches of the month.

⚔️ Bravery by Radek Ignatów — Kickstarter, launching June 9. Card-based wilderness survival game. Not live yet but on the Launchpad as upcoming.

🃏 Flipping Fortune by Georg Fischer — Kickstarter, ends May 26. Quirky push-your-luck tarot card game with deckbuilding. Last week of the campaign + bonus card for backers!

🏴‍☠️ Roll 4 MORE Rum! by Florian Fiedler — Gamefound, preview. Expansion to the beloved Roll4Rum series. Deeper mechanics, specialized crew, career-based ship command. No date yet.

🐉 Dungeon Legacy: A Roll & Crawl PnP Adventure by Nezih Savı — Kickstarter, ends June 10. Fully funded! Roll-and-crawl dungeon adventure with just sheets and three dice.

👑 Crown & Courage by Petter Schanke Olsen — Kickstarter, launches June 2. Preview PnP available now. Play as the King of Norway escaping the Germans. Premium pledge includes all previous games.

🪢 Rope Walkers by A. R. Curry — Kickstarter, ends May 25. Solo dice-rolling pocket game about balance and bravery. Faith Dickey variant card announced! About 4 days left.

🐼 Red Panda Surprise by Dylan Coyle — Backerkit, ends May 28. Fast card game where red pandas try to scare each other but are too cute! Physical Backerkit campaign running alongside.

🤖 Iron Protocol: Exodus by NukaZombee Games — Kickstarter, ends May 31. Halfway through with 340+ mech pilots! Compact solo sci-fi tactical combat. 10 days left.

🧟 Hidden Realms: The Mummy's Tomb by Spiros Kallos — Gamefound, ended May 18. Late pledge now open! Single-page roll-and-write dungeon crawl. Polyomino mapping, D&D-inspired.

🎭 Charles Ward / Circat — 3 games at Tokyo Game Market (free PnP). EFS: The Universal Voting Game is on the Launchpad. The other 2 Circat games aren't listed yet.
