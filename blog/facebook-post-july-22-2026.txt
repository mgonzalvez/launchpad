5 new additions to the Launchpad today. Three are already live on Kickstarter, two are previews worth watching.

Medieval Command: Battle of Varna, 1444 (Martin Melbardis) launches today — solo PnP wargame about the final European Crusade.

Master of Coin (Postmark Games) is a coin-flipping kingdom management game. Real coins, real decisions.

Battlestations: Lightspeed (Jeffrey Siadek) — co-op starship adventure for 1-4 players.

Shadowdark Triple Feature (Joseph R. Lewis) — three one-shot adventure zines for Shadowdark RPG.

Thief in Da Building (Michał Kujawa) — asymmetric chase game, police vs thief.

Plus previews: The Undervaults (solo roll-and-write dungeon crawler with auto-bookkeeping) and Miskatonic Expeditions (Lovecraftian tile-laying roguelite).
