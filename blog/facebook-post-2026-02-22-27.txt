What's New on the Launchpad This Week of Feb. 22-27, 2026

Pressure is up at the front of the page this week. The nearest deadlines belong to Red Dungeon and ORMAN, both closing tomorrow. If you've been sitting on those, this is the moment to decide.

Red Dungeon remains one of the cleaner pitches on the board right now: compact, standard-deck friendly, and laser focused on getting dungeon tension out of minimal materials. ORMAN still stands apart because its plant-and-grow identity gives it a different rhythm than the usual abstract roll-and-write lane, which makes it feel fresher than a lot of quick-sheet projects.

Red Dungeon
https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/timetodice/red-dungeon

ORMAN: A Roll & Plant Print and Play Game
https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/keepersoffun/orman-a-roll-and-plant-print-and-play-game

Right behind them, Essence: The Grim Expeditions is still one of the most ambitious campaign-style systems currently live, and The Black Dahlia Case is there if you want something more theatrical and event-driven than the strategy-heavy projects dominating the page this week.

Essence: The Grim Expeditions
https://gamefound.com/en/projects/florianfiedler/essence-the-grim-expeditions

The Black Dahlia Case
