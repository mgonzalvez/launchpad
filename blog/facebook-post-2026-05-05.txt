What's new on the Launchpad today?

Mïnomancer: Roll em Bones! is finally live on Gamefound. Jack Chapman's solo roll-and-write dungeon crawler where you raise a skeletal army, arrange polyomino formations, and fight 15+ bosses across three dungeon levels. One of the most anticipated PnP launches of the month:
https://gamefound.com/en/projects/beaverlicious-games/minomancer---the-roll--write-dungeon-crawler-dynamic-pnp-of-the-month--gamebook

Play With Fire - The Card Game launched today on Kickstarter. Strategic push-your-luck elimination for 2-6 players. Accumulate mana, cast spells, strike opponents. Ends May 26:
https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/umbrapublishing/play-with-fire-the-card-game

Cryptid Creek: Build a Legend is a competitive card game where you craft fake cryptids from mismatched body parts. Only one can win, but if the reality TV crew exposes the hoax, everyone loses. $3 PnP tier. Ends May 28:
https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/mayhemdelight/cryptid-creek-build-a-legend?ref=9d7bci

Shinobi Spy & Supply from Orca Island Games is a small card game about competing shops selling gear to Japanese fantasy spies. $3 PnP. Ends May 31:
https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/orcaislandgames/shinobi-spy-and-supply

EFS: The Universal Voting Game went live yesterday. Aliens vote on Earth stuff and try to find the human. Short, light, silly party game. Late pledge available through June 4:
https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/ex1stgames/efs-the-universal-voting-game

Nine Nights Siege is a tactical solo/co-op PnP game that designer Alex Ivanov has been working on for a year. Ends May 25:
https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/lanternling/nine-nights-siege

The live rail is at eight campaigns now. Iron Protocol: Exodus, Flock and Squabble, Bee The Buzz, Greetings from Earth, Nothing But Net, Hidden Realms: The Mummy's Tomb, Sole Survivor, and Pocket Puffins are all still running.
